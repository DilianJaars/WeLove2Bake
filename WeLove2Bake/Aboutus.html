Our story

Baked with heart, right here in Cape Town.

WeLoved2Bake started in a home kitchen in Observatory and grew, one custom order at a time, into a small studio that still bakes everything by hand.

Meet the baker

Hi, I'm Thandiwe — founder of WeLoved2Bake

"I never wanted to bake the same cake twice. Every order tells me something about the person it's for, and that's what I'm actually baking — the story, not just the sponge."
Thandiwe Nkosi, Founder & Head Baker
What we believe

Our values in the kitchen

Made from scratch, every time

No premixes, no shortcuts — sponges, fillings and buttercream are made fresh for each order.

Personal to you

We design around your story first, and figure out the technique second — not the other way round.

Rooted in Cape Town

Ingredients are sourced from local suppliers where we can, and orders are baked to order for pickup or delivery nearby.

Community-minded

We donate leftover-safe offcuts and small orders to a local shelter each month, and keep a sliding scale for school fundraisers.

How we got here

A short history of a small kitchen

2019

Baked the first order at the kitchen table

A birthday cake for a neighbor's daughter turned into three more orders that same week.

2021

Moved into a small home studio

Registered the business and set up a dedicated baking space in Observatory.

2023

Delivered our 100th wedding cake

Word of mouth carried us into tiered wedding cakes across the Southern Suburbs.

Today

Still a small, hands-on team

Every order is still baked, filled and decorated by hand, Monday to Friday.
