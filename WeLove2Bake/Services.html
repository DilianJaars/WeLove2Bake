Good to know

Common questions

How far in advance should I order?

2 weeks for celebration cakes, 6–8 weeks for wedding and large tiered orders. Rush orders are sometimes possible — just ask.

Do you cater to allergies or dietary needs?

Yes — eggless and gluten-friendly options are available on request, made in a kitchen that also handles nuts and gluten.

What areas do you deliver to?

We deliver across Cape Town's Southern Suburbs and City Bowl; further areas are quoted individually.

How do I pay?

A deposit secures your date, with the balance due on collection or before delivery. EFT and card both work.

Ready to start designing?

Send us your date and a few details — we'll reply during business hours, Monday to Friday, 8am–3pm.
