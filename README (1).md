## Project Overview

**WeLove2Bake** is a responsive, five-page website for a small home-based baking business in Cape Town. The business specialises in **personalised cakes and cupcakes** for birthdays, weddings, baby showers and corporate events, and also offers a range of **low-calorie treats** for customers who want to enjoy baked goods without the guilt.

Before this project, WeLove2Bake relied on word of mouth and social media messages to take orders. This made it hard for customers to see the full product range, understand the services on offer, or get in touch in an organised way. The website gives the business a professional online home where customers can learn about the baker, browse products and services, and send an enquiry or order request.

The site is built with **HTML5**, **CSS3** and a small amount of **JavaScript**, is version-controlled with **Git**, and is stored in a remote **GitHub** repository. It contains **5 pages** and uses **7 images**.

---

## Website Goals and Objectives

**Primary goal:** Showcase WeLove2Bake's products and services and make it easy for customers in Cape Town to get in touch and place personalised or low-calorie orders.

**Objectives**

1. Present the brand with a warm, inviting look using consistent colours, typography and imagery.
2. Clearly separate what WeLove2Bake sells (Products page) from what it offers (Services page).
3. Highlight the personalisation options and the low-calorie range as the business's key selling points.
4. Build trust through an About Us page telling the story behind the business.
5. Provide a simple contact and order enquiry form so customers can describe their occasion, flavour, size and date required.
6. Ensure the site is accessible and works well on mobile devices, since most customers browse on their phones.
7. Keep the code clean and well-organised, with regular, descriptive commits to the repository.

---

## Key Features and Functionality

- **Responsive layout** – adapts to mobile, tablet and desktop using Flexbox, CSS Grid and media queries.
- **Consistent navigation and footer** – shared across all five pages, with links to social media.
- **Home page hero section** – eye-catching cake photo, tagline ("Baked with love, made just for you") and a call-to-action button.
- **Services page** – personalised cake design, custom cupcake boxes for events, low-calorie baking, and collection/delivery within Cape Town.
- **Products page** – cakes, cupcakes and low-calorie treats displayed as cards with descriptions, flavours and "from" prices in Rand (R).
- **About Us page** – the story of the business, the baker's passion for baking and the reason behind the low-calorie range.
- **Contact Us page** – enquiry/order form (name, contact details, occasion, flavour, size, required date) with client-side validation, plus location and social media details.
- **7 optimised images** – used across the site with descriptive alt text.
- **Accessibility** – semantic HTML, labelled form fields and sufficient colour contrast.
- **Cross-browser tested** – checked in Chrome, Firefox and Edge.
